This is a template article. To publish a new one, copy this file inside
`src/content/news/`, rename it to something like `my-article-title.mdx`, and
edit the fields at the top (title, date, excerpt, author).

## Writing content

Everything below the `---` block is regular Markdown. You can write
paragraphs, add headings with `##`, **bold text**, _italic text_, and
[links](https://csuf.edu).

New files automatically show up on the [News page](/news) — sorted by date,
newest first. No `.tsx` editing required.
